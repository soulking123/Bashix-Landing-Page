# DESIGN.md — Bashix Engineering Platform Design Direction

## 1. Brand Identity & Product Scope
- **Name:** Bashix (`bashix.id`)
- **Location:** Jakarta, Indonesia
- **Core Offering:** Turnkey physical industrial IoT hardware, edge compute units, sensor nodes, and custom electronic engineering services.
- **Personality:** Tangible industrial craftsmanship, rugged technical reliability, precision instrumentation. Inspired by Teenage Engineering, Framework, and Keyence.
- **Tone:** Professional, direct, grounded, evidence-driven. Zero generic AI marketing hype.

---

## 2. Antislop Dials
- **ENERGY:** 3 (Clear technical hierarchy, high-contrast typography, distinct functional sections)
- **RHYTHM:** 3 (Varied density: technical spec sheets, interactive hardware schematics, tactile product catalog)
- **MOTION:** 2 (Crisp functional feedback: modal transitions, cart drawer slide, tab switches; no distracting particle storms or full-page glow animations)

---

## 3. Color Palette: Precision Titanium & Carbon
- **Background Primary:** `#101317` (Deep matte charcoal carbon)
- **Surface Panels:** `#181D24` (Machined titanium dark card surface)
- **Surface Raised / Active:** `#222832` (Interactive card hover & active tabs)
- **Borders:** `#2D3545` (Crisp 1px structural hairline borders)
- **Border Active:** `#4B5568`
- **Text Primary:** `#F3F5F7` (Crisp titanium white, > 14:1 contrast)
- **Text Secondary:** `#94A3B8` (Legible slate silver, > 5:1 contrast)
- **Text Muted:** `#64748B` (Technical annotations and metadata)
- **Accent Primary / Hardware Titanium:** `#E2E8F0` (Metallic steel & high-contrast white)
- **Accent Status / Telemetry Amber:** `#F59E0B` (Tactile amber for power, GPIO, and active bus indicators)
- **Accent Hardware Green:** `#10B981` (In-stock and online operational status)
- **Zero Saturated/Neon Blue:** All controls use refined titanium monochrome with tactical amber highlights.

---

## 4. Typography
- **Technical Headings:** `Space Grotesk` (Geometric, clean technical authority)
- **Interface & Body:** `Inter` (Standard for high legibility at 14px to 16px)
- **Hardware Pinouts, SKUs & Code:** `JetBrains Mono` (High-contrast monospace for pin designations and terminal wiring)

---

## 5. Anti-Slop Guidelines (Explicit Revisions)
1. **No Em Dashes (R-02):** Zero em dashes (`—`) in copy. Use periods, colons, or commas.
2. **No Fake Statistics (R-17 & R-36):** Real technical specifications: operating voltages (9-36V DC), temperature ratings (-40°C to +85°C), processor clock speeds, and memory configurations.
3. **No Fake Terminal Window (R-08 & R-10):** Real interactive **Hardware Port & Schematic Viewer** (inspect real I/O: Gigabit Ethernet, RS-485 Modbus, CAN-FD, Power terminal).
4. **No Gratuitous Blur Blobs (R-01 & R-07):** Clean, solid, dark matte charcoal and titanium surfaces.
5. **No Decorative Button Arrows (R-15):** Buttons state clear functional verbs ("Browse Hardware Catalog", "Add to Cart", "Contact Sales").
6. **No Capsule Eyebrow Badges (R-11):** Clean section context labels.
7. **No Unverified Trust Badges (R-36):** Real hardware form factors (DIN Rail EN 50022, IP67 sealed, M.2 2280).
8. **Real Navigation (R-24 & R-26):** Every link in the header anchors to a real, rendered section.
